# Zaria Capital brand kit (web)

Everything here comes from Zaria Capital's own house materials (its master deck template and the logo files inside it). Nothing is invented.

## Logo
- `logo/svg/zaria-mark.svg`: the Z mark alone. Clean geometric rebuild of the mark from the template files (matches the original raster to within edge antialiasing). Use for favicon, header on small screens, section ornaments.
- `logo/svg/zaria-logo-horizontal.svg`: Z beside ZARIACAPITAL. Primary header logo on desktop.
- `logo/svg/zaria-logo-stacked.svg`: Z over ZARIACAPITAL. Footer, splash, OG image.
- `*-mono-dark.svg`: single ink #1B331B. `*-white.svg`: all white, for use on the dark green band only.
- `logo/png/`: PNG exports of every SVG.
- The wordmark letters in the lockups are traced from the template's raster artwork. They are faithful but not the designer's master vectors. The template artwork was exported from Illustrator, so a master file exists: if Zaria can supply it (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG), use it instead.
- Inks: upper stroke and wordmark #1B331B; lower stroke #495C49.
- Rules: never redraw, recolour (other than the supplied mono/white variants), outline, stretch, rotate or add effects. Minimum clear space around the logo: the height of the Z's top bar on every side. Minimum width: horizontal lockup 140px, mark 24px.
- `source-originals/` holds the untouched files from the template for reference. `zaria-mark-with-rule-deck-original.png` includes the vertical rule and diamond used in decks; that ornament is deck furniture, not part of the logo.

## Favicon
`favicon/`: favicon.svg (switches to a light version on dark browser chrome), favicon.ico (16/32), favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180), icon-192.png, icon-512.png, site.webmanifest.

## Colour, type, shape
See `tokens.css` for exact values and usage. In short: white ground; dark green (#2A4520) carries structure; gold (#B28B33) is the single accent, used once per view at most; square corners; no shadows; no gradients; hairline rules. The house typeface is Perpetua (Monotype, licensed).

## Imagery
`imagery/madrid-gran-via-bw.jpg`: the black and white aerial of central Madrid (Gran Vía and the Metrópolis building) used on Zaria's deck covers. Licence status unknown: confirm Zaria holds web rights before publishing it. House style keeps all photography black and white.
